# Code of Conduct

Pay On Arc follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for impact.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make contributors feel unsafe.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other action when behavior violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
